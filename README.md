## Why am I making this?
I'm working together with Hack Club to host Haven Nottingham, Nottingham's first youth game jam. 
I wanted our attendees to have an event that feels empowering as well as have fun mementos to take home that remind them of the event.
So we partnered with JLCPCB and easy EDA who have graciously provided the funding to produce these badges!

## What does it do?
I wanted to make something I had never really seen done before, a PCB badge that requires no cables, no batteries and allows the user to stream video and power to it just using their phone. Allowing them to express themselves around the event in whatever way they may want. As our attendees will mainly be beginners it integrates with a custom web app, that makes it super simple and intuitive for people to use the badges. 

## How it works
The NFC in your phone can produce up to 3.3V, the badge takes the energy produced and stores it in capacitors within the badge. These act almost like mini batteries meaning that the amount of power required to change the pixels on the E-Ink display can stay consistent for the amount of time required to render the image. Images are transmitted and streamed to the badge via NFC to the inbuilt low power micro controller. It then translates those signals and sends them on to the dedicated E-Ink display driver and then onto the display.
